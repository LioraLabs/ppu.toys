title: A sound effect is a short one-shot, from the built-in bank first and a WAV sample when that fails
codes: []
---
An effect answers an action, so it is short: most are under a quarter second, and the longest (an
explosion, a fanfare) under a second. It borrows a voice from the song for that long
([[voices-for-songs-and-effects]]), so a short effect leaves the music whole.

Reach for the built-in bank first, shaped by its preset: `pluck` or `bell` high and short for a pickup or a
menu tick, `tom` or `kick` low for a hit, `crash` or `snare` pitched down for an explosion, a melodic
sound keyed twice a fourth or fifth apart for a coin. The note is its pitch, `len` how long a looping
sound rings ([[looping-sample-stops-after-len]]), `adsr` whether it snaps or fades, and `vol` its
place under the song.

When nothing in the bank is the sound (a rising jump, a laser falling to a floor pitch, a voice), make it a
WAV, generated by [[ppu-sfx|ppu sfx]] or recorded, and use it as a sample ([[samples-are-sources]]): mono, short, and
counted in the sound RAM the samples and echo share. Name effects as cues so the game plays them by name
([[sound-effects-with-bank-and-cues]]).
