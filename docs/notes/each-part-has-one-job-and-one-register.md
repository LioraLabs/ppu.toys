title: Each part of a song has one job and one register, and the bass starts at a third of the lead's vol

Eight voices are the whole band, shared with the game's sound effects
([[voices-for-songs-and-effects]]), so every part earns its voice with one job in one register:

- **Lead** carries the tune, C5 to C6: `lead` is the bright buzzy one, `flute` and `bell` are softer.
- **Harmony** answers or supports it a third or sixth under, or an octave down. Two parts a third
  apart at the same level fight: move one an octave before turning it down.
- **Bass** holds the root, C1 to C3. `bass` is far louder than `lead` at the same row `vol`: start its
  rows near a third of the lead's `vol`, then read the per-sound levels.
- **Drums** mark time: `kick` and `snare` the beat, `hat` the subdivisions, `crash` the arrivals.

A row is one sound at one pitch with its own `vol` and pan; a note's `vel` is the accent on top. The
check's per-sound loudness should read lead on top, bass 2 to 4 dB under it, drums under the bass
([[compose-by-measuring-what-you-cannot-hear]]). Pan the lead and its echo apart and keep bass and kick
in the centre. A part that has no job yet is a voice taken from the effects.
