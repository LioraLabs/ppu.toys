title: A chord is an arpeggio of very short notes on one voice, or offbeat stabs under a busy lead

Three voices can hold a real triad, but that is three of the eight the song shares with the game's
sound effects ([[voices-for-songs-and-effects]]). Two cheaper shapes carry a chord:

- **Arpeggio.** One voice cycles the chord's notes in very short notes: 2 units each (at 48 to the
  beat) is a sixtieth of a second at 150 BPM. It reads as a chord with the chip's shimmer, on one voice.
- **Stabs.** Under a busy lead, short chord hits on the offbeats, or in a 3+3+2 push across the bar,
  each struck and left to die away ([[envelopes-belong-to-sounds]]). A held arpeggio under a busy
  melody buzzes; stabs leave gaps the lead breathes through.

The check counts held clashes (a semitone, tritone or major seventh sounding together for at least three quarters of a beat);
arpeggio steps are too short to count, so a wrong note inside an arpeggio is found by ear, or by reading
the rows ([[compose-by-measuring-what-you-cannot-hear]]).
