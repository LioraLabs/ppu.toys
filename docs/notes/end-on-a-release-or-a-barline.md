id: end-on-a-release-or-a-barline
kind: concept
title: A song that ends on a long release or a clean stop on a barline sounds finished; a loop ends leading home
codes: []
---
A song played once (`loop = false` on its [[score]]) ends where its last pattern does. Two endings read as
intent: a final chord held long and fading while it is held (a long note, and `r` low but not 0 on its
sound's preset, [[envelopes-belong-to-sounds]]: at key-off the chip cuts what is left in milliseconds), or every part stopping together on a barline, often with a hit on the
last downbeat. A note cut off mid-ring, or parts trailing out one by one, reads as a song that broke.

A game tune that loops has no ending: it has a seam. End the last bar on the chord that leads back to the
first, leave a short rest before the seam so the restart is clean, and put an intro that plays once before
the song's loop start. A long release across the seam is cut when the loop restarts on that voice
([[song-voices-steal-the-oldest-note]]).
